17 November 2026 · 立冬 (7–21 Nov) · Start of Winter

Lidong: dumplings, and the storing begins

Winter begins now — not with snow, but with storing.

What happens

Today is in Lidong, the Start of Winter (7–21 November). The old calendar says winter begins now — not with snow, but with storing. The harvest is in, the days are short, and the work of the season is to put things away and build up strength. In the north, Lidong has a taste: dumplings. The saying goes, “If you don't hold a bowl of dumplings at Lidong, your ears will freeze and nobody will care” — the story is that the physician Zhang Zhongjing made dumplings stuffed with mutton and herbs for people whose ears had frozen in the cold, and the custom stuck. Whether or not you believe the story, the habit is real: at Lidong, families sit down to dumplings, hot and fresh, and winter starts with a full table.

Try it

  1. Make or buy dumplings today. Eat them hot, with black vinegar.
  2. Put one thing away for winter — the summer clothes, the light bedding. The season of storing has begun.

Questions

Why dumplings at Lidong?

The legend says the physician Zhang Zhongjing made dumplings stuffed with mutton and herbs for people whose ears had frozen in the cold, and the custom stuck. The custom is northern; in the south, Lidong is more often marked with tonic soups and warming food. Either way, the point is the same: eat warm, eat well, store up for winter.

Words to know

立冬 (lì dōng)
The Start of Winter — the nineteenth of the twenty-four solar terms.
冬藏 (dōng cáng)
Winter storing — the season's work: put things away, build strength.
