Calendar

The old calendar, month by month — November 2026 to December 2027. Each day shows three things: the Gregorian date, the lunar date, and — in vermilion — the solar term or festival, when there is one.

How to read it

  • The big number is the Gregorian day.
  • The small grey line is the lunar date; on the first day of a lunar month it shows the month name instead.
  • A vermilion line means a solar term or a festival falls on that day.
  • Today — 17 November 2026 — is marked in vermilion, with the word "Today".

Lunar dates are computed from the traditional calendar and cross-checked against public almanacs. Solar term dates follow the Hong Kong Observatory's almanac. 2027 has no leap month; the next leap month is the fifth month of 2028.
