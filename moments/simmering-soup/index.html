16:00 · 申时 · 15–17 · 膀胱经当令

Start the soup — seasonal, by the solar terms

A soup simmers for hours — it is care, cooked slowly.

What happens

At four in the afternoon, the soup goes on. Meat or bones, a few dried ingredients, cold water in a clay pot — then hours of the gentlest simmer. Nobody watches it. It just sits there on the lowest flame, doing its work, while the afternoon goes on around it. 煲汤 (bāo tāng) is care, cooked slowly: the point is not speed but extraction, and the recipe follows the twenty-four solar terms — what simmers in summer is not what simmers in winter.

Why this hour

Soup starts in 申时 (shēn shí), 3–5 p.m. — the bladder's hours in the old clock, the water hours. The traditional view pairs the afternoon with water and rest before the evening meal. The practical reason is the same: a soup that needs hours must start now to be on the table at 18:30.

Try it

  1. Pick a soup for the season — light and clear in summer, richer with root vegetables in winter.
  2. Start with cold water and bring it to a boil, then skim off the foam.
  3. Turn the heat to the lowest simmer and leave it for 2–3 hours.
  4. Salt at the end, not the start.
  5. Serve with dinner — the soup started at 16:00 is on the table at 18:30.

Words to know

煲汤 (bāo tāng)
Slow-simmered soup — hours on the lowest flame.
二十四节气 (èr shí sì jié qì)
The twenty-four solar terms — the old farming calendar that decides the recipe.
火候 (huǒ hòu)
Heat control — the knack of the flame.
砂锅 (shā guō)
Clay pot — the traditional soup pot.

Questions

How is 煲汤 different from ordinary soup?

Time. A quick soup is boiled and served; 煲汤 simmers for hours on the lowest flame. The point is slow extraction — flavour and nourishment drawn out gradually.

What goes into a seasonal soup?

It follows the twenty-four solar terms. Summer soups are light and clearing; winter soups are richer, often with root vegetables and dried ingredients. The season decides, not the cook's whim.

Do I need a special pot?

A clay pot (砂锅) is traditional because it holds gentle heat well. Any heavy pot with a lid works — the low flame matters more than the pot.

When do you eat it?

With dinner. The soup started at 16:00 is on the table at 18:30, alongside the dishes.

Is soup a dish or a drink?

Both. It arrives with the meal and is eaten with it — sipped from a small bowl between bites, not as a starter and not as an afterthought.
