The magazine: one term at a time

A magazine that follows the old calendar. Every day one short piece; every solar term a biweekly issue; every month a monthly issue. Coming soon.

How it works

  1. Every day, one piece. Today's date, today's solar term, one thing that belongs to this season — about 200 words, one question, one word. Free, on the site and on social media. See Daily.
  2. Every solar term, a biweekly issue. Fifteen days of pieces become one issue, gathering everything that belongs to that term: what to plant, what to eat, how to eat, how to look after yourself, what to take to build strength, which tea, which soup. $4.99.
  3. Every month, a monthly issue. The two term issues of the month, plus a month overview and a month plan. $7.99.

What's in each issue

Seven things, every term: what to plant · what to eat · how to eat · how to look after yourself · what to take to build strength · which tea · which soup. Plus the moment of the term, questions and answers, and words to know. Everything is public, checkable fact or clearly labelled tradition — never medical advice.

When it starts

The first issues arrive only if the gate passes on 5 December (the Tai Chi guide test and 100 visits to the homepage). If it passes: the Major Snow issue on 7 December, the Winter Solstice issue on 22 December, and the January monthly on 1 January 2027. If it doesn't, the magazine doesn't start.
